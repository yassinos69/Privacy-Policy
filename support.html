How {{APP_NAME}} works

Add each bill or subscription with its amount, due date, and how often it repeats. {{APP_NAME}} keeps the cycle rolling forward and reminds you before each payment lands, so nothing renews behind your back.

Your bills are stored on your device — not on our servers. Signing in is optional and only secures your account for restoring it later.

01 — Reminders

Reminders aren't arriving

Nine times out of ten this is an iOS permission or a Focus mode, not the app. Work through these in order:

Check notification permission

Open Settings → Notifications → {{APP_NAME}} and make sure Allow Notifications is on, with Lock Screen, Notification Center, and Banners all enabled. If you tapped "Don't Allow" the first time you launched the app, this is where you turn it back on.

Check Focus and Scheduled Summary

Under Settings → Focus, add {{APP_NAME}} to the allowed apps for any Focus you use, including Sleep and Do Not Disturb. Then check Settings → Notifications → Scheduled Summary — if {{APP_NAME}} is in the summary, reminders get held back and delivered in a batch instead of at the due time.

Check the reminder time on the bill

Open the bill and confirm the reminder is set for a day and time still in the future. A reminder scheduled for a moment that has already passed won't fire, and a bill marked as paid stops reminding until the next cycle begins.

Check Low Power Mode and time zone

Low Power Mode can delay background delivery. Also make sure Settings → General → Date & Time → Set Automatically is on — a manually set clock or a recent time-zone change can shift when reminders fire.

Still nothing after all four? Email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, and a screenshot of Settings → Notifications → {{APP_NAME}}.
02 — Sign-in

Can't sign in with Apple or Google

Sign-in is handled entirely by Apple and Google, so we can't reset anything on our side — but these usually clear it up:

  1. Confirm you're online, then force-quit the app and reopen it.
  2. For Apple: check Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple. If {{APP_NAME}} is listed, tap it and choose Stop using Apple ID, then sign in again from the app.
  3. For Google: make sure the right account is selected on the account chooser — a work or school account may be blocked by its administrator.
  4. Sign in with the same provider you used originally. Apple and Google accounts are separate identities, even with the same email address.

If you used Apple's Hide My Email, your account is tied to the relay address Apple generated. That still works — just sign in with Apple again rather than searching for your real email.

03 — Purchases

A purchase isn't unlocking

Payments run through the App Store, so your receipt lives with Apple. To restore access:

  1. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID that made the purchase.
  2. Open the app's settings and tap Restore purchases.
  3. If nothing changes, force-quit the app, reopen it, and restore once more.

Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, not to your device, so they carry over to any iPhone you sign into with that account. If you were charged and the feature still won't unlock, send us the receipt Apple emailed you and we'll sort it out.

04 — Billing

Cancelling, refunds, and plan changes

Apple manages all billing, so these are done in your Apple account rather than in the app:

  • Cancel or change a plan: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → {{APP_NAME}}. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Request a refund: visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.
  • Billing history: also under Report a Problem, or in the receipts Apple emails you.

We can't issue refunds or see your payment details ourselves — only Apple can. Tell us anyway if something went wrong, so we can fix the cause.

05 — Devices

New phone, or bills that vanished

Your bills are stored on your device. When you move to a new iPhone, restore it from an iCloud or Mac backup and your data comes across with it. Setting the new phone up as new starts {{APP_NAME}} empty.

If bills disappeared on a device you've been using all along, check that you haven't deleted and reinstalled the app — reinstalling gives you a clean slate. Sign back in first before re-entering anything, then contact us if the data doesn't return.

06 — Your data

Deleting your data or account

You can remove individual bills at any time from inside the app, or delete the app to clear its local data from your device. To disconnect sign-in, revoke {{APP_NAME}}'s access from your Apple or Google account settings.

We collect no personal data, so there's nothing on our side to erase — details are in the privacy policy.

07 — Feedback

Report a bug or request a feature

Bug reports get fixed faster with a bit of detail. When you write in, include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About).
  • The app version, shown in the app's settings screen.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording if you can catch it.

Feature ideas are welcome too — tell us the problem you're trying to solve, not just the feature. It usually leads somewhere better.
