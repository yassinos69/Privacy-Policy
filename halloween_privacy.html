Halloween Photo Booth AR

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026 · Effective date: August 15, 2026

Halloween Photo Booth AR ("the App," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what the App does and doesn't do with your information, in plain language.

What We Collect

The App lets you capture photos, videos, and Boomerang clips with real-time AR filters. Everything you capture is stored locally on your device only, in the App's own private storage — we do not upload, transmit, or store your photos or videos on any server we control, and the App never requests access to your device's Photos library at all. The App requests Camera access (to power AR capture) and, when you record a video, Microphone access (for its audio); you control both at any time in iOS Settings.

What We Don't Collect

The App has no accounts — there's no sign-up, login, email, or password. We don't use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind, so no usage data about you is collected or sent anywhere. We don't access your contacts, location, or microphone beyond what a video you choose to record needs. We don't display ads, and we don't sell or share data with anyone, because we don't collect any to begin with.

In-App Purchases

The App is currently entirely free — no in-app purchases are offered. If that changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first, and any purchase would be processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we would never receive or store your payment details.

Sharing Your Content

If you use the Share feature, your photo or video is handed to your device's native share sheet and sent directly to whatever destination you choose (Messages, Instagram, etc.) — that transfer happens between your device and the destination app, not through us.

Data Storage & Security

Your captures stay on your device, protected by iOS's app sandboxing and your device's own security (passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID, if enabled). We don't operate a server that stores any of your personal content.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children — there's no account system to collect it through in the first place.

Your Choices

You can turn off Camera or Microphone access at any time in iOS Settings (this will limit what the App can do). You can delete any captured photo or video at any time from the Gallery tab. Deleting the App removes everything it stored on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
