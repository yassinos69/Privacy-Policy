Frequently Asked Questions

The AR filters aren't tracking my face correctly. What can I do?

Make sure you're in a well-lit space with your face fully visible to the camera, then try repositioning or restarting the App. If it still doesn't track, force-quit and relaunch the App.

Can I use the App without an internet connection?

Yes — camera capture, AR filters, your gallery, and editing all work fully offline. You only need a connection to share content externally.

How do I change the App's language?

Go to Settings → Language and choose from English, Spanish, French, Japanese, or German.

How do I delete my photos or videos?

Open the Gallery tab, select the item, and choose Delete. This only removes it from your device — we never had a copy anywhere else.

Does the App collect my personal data?

No. The App has no accounts and collects no analytics or tracking data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I delete all my data?

Everything the App stores lives on your device, so deleting the App removes all of it.

Reporting a Bug

Please include the following when you email us:

  1. Your device model and iOS version
  2. Steps to reproduce the issue
  3. What you expected to happen vs. what actually happened
  4. A screenshot or screen recording, if possible
