Super Hero City Protector Privacy Policy
Privacy Policy

Super Hero City Protector

Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Applies to the iOS and Android versions of the game

The short version

  • You do not need an account, and the game does not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device and usage data to show and measure those ads.
  • On iPhone and iPad we ask for your permission before tracking you for ads. You can say no, or change your mind later in Settings.
  • The game is for players aged 13 and over.

Who we are

Super Hero City Protector (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is a mobile action game developed by Yassine, an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game and its advertising partner collect, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us

None. The game has no sign-up, login, chat or user profiles. Your game progress, settings and scores are stored on your device only and are not sent to us.

Information collected automatically by our advertising partner

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads. When ads load or play, Google and its ad partners may collect the following:

DataExamplesWhy
Device identifiersAdvertising ID (IDFA on iOS, Android Advertising ID)Show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad
Approximate locationCoarse location derived from your IP addressShow ads relevant to your region
Ad interactionsAds shown, tapped or watchedAd delivery, measurement and reporting
DiagnosticsCrash reports, load times, device model, operating system versionKeep the ad service stable and fast

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you personally. It is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy (see “Third-party services” below).

How information is used

  • To show ads in the game, which is how we keep the game free.
  • To measure how ads perform and prevent ad fraud.
  • To find and fix crashes and keep ads loading reliably.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data to build profiles of our own.

Tracking and your choices

Where the law or the platform requires it, we ask for your permission first.

  • iPhone and iPad: the game shows Apple’s tracking prompt on first launch. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are not personalized using your advertising ID. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and similar regions: the game shows a consent form from Google before any ads load. You can accept, decline or manage your choices, and you can reopen the form later where the game offers a privacy options entry.

If you decline personalization you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

Third-party services

The game includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google’s handling of data is described in these documents:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and AdSense data privacy information

AdMob may share data with the advertising networks that bid to show ads in the game. Each network has its own privacy policy.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will help you resolve it. Parents can also limit ad tracking on the child’s device with the platform settings described above.

Data retention and security

Game progress stays on your device until you delete the game or its data. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google’s retention practices. We do not run servers that store your personal information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area and the UK, Quebec’s privacy law, or California’s CCPA), you may have the right to know what data is held about you, ask for access, correction or deletion, withdraw consent, and object to certain processing. Because we do not hold personal information about you ourselves, most requests are best made through your device’s advertising settings and, for data held by Google, through your Google account privacy controls at myaccount.google.com. You can also contact us and we will do what we can to help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game adds a feature or a new service. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant we will make the update visible in the game or on the store page.
