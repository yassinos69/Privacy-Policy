01 — Who this covers

About this policy

{{APP_NAME}}: Bills Reminder ("the app") helps you keep track of recurring bills and subscriptions and reminds you before they are due. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

By using {{APP_NAME}}, you agree to this policy. If you don't agree with it, please don't use the app.

02 — What we collect

We don't collect your data

{{APP_NAME}} does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to us. We run no analytics, no tracking SDKs, no advertising networks, and no profiling of any kind.

Everything you enter — bill names, amounts, due dates, billing cycles, categories, notes, and reminder settings — is stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, that data may be backed up to your own iCloud account under Apple's terms. We have no access to it.

Because your bills live on your device, uninstalling the app removes them. Keep a device or iCloud backup if you want to restore them later.
03 — Sign-in

Signing in with Apple or Google

The app offers optional sign-in with Apple and Google so you can secure your account and restore it on a new device. Authentication is performed entirely by Apple and Google — we never see or handle your password.

When you sign in, the provider returns a basic account identifier and, depending on your choice, an email address. This is used only to identify your account inside the app. We do not build a profile from it, enrich it, share it, or use it for marketing.

Apple's Hide My Email is fully supported: if you use it, we only ever see the relay address Apple generates for you.

  • Apple: review or revoke access under Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.
  • Google: review or revoke access in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps.

Your use of these sign-in services is also governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

04 — Payments

In-app purchases

{{APP_NAME}} offers optional in-app purchases. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. Your card details, billing address, and payment history never pass through the app and are never visible to us.

The app only receives a receipt from Apple confirming whether a purchase is active, so it can unlock the corresponding features. Refunds, subscription management, and cancellations are handled by Apple in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

05 — Reminders

Notifications

Bill reminders are delivered as local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing about your bills is sent to a server to trigger them. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings — the app will keep working, it just won't remind you.

06 — Advertising

No ads, ever

{{APP_NAME}} contains no advertising and no ad identifiers. We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), and the app never presents the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track you across apps or websites.

07 — Your rights

Your choices and control

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to export, correct, or erase. You remain in control at all times:

  • Delete individual bills or all app data from inside the app.
  • Delete the app to remove its local data from your device.
  • Revoke Apple or Google sign-in access from your account settings.

If you are in the EU/EEA, the UK, or California, you still have rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it, and we will never discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right. Write to us if you want to exercise one.

08 — Children

Children's privacy

{{APP_NAME}} is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. As the app collects no personal data at all, no such information can be gathered through normal use.

09 — Security

Keeping things safe

Your data stays on your device, protected by iOS's built-in encryption and your device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. Because we operate no servers holding your bills, there is no central database of your finances to breach.

10 — Updates

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date at the top revised. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect. Continuing to use {{APP_NAME}} after an update means you accept the revised policy.
